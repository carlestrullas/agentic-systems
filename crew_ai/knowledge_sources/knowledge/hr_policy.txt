Lumen Analytics - Leave and Attendance Policy (version 3, January 2026)

1. Paid leave: every full-time employee is entitled to 23 working days of paid leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a proportional number of days.
2. New employees: paid leave can be used once the probation period of 60 days has been completed. Days accrued during probation are kept.
3. Carry-over: up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used before 31 March.
4. Sick leave: notify your manager and People Ops before 9:30 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day.
5. Remote work: employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote weeks need written approval from the team lead.
6. Requests: leave of 5 or more consecutive days must be requested in the HR portal at least 3 weeks in advance.
7. Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for every parent, which can be taken in blocks during the first 12 months.
